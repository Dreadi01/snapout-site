Snap Out — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

Overview

Snap Out is a screen-time and digital wellness application built using Expo and React Native, designed to help users build healthier device habits and reduce screen friction. We respect your privacy and believe your personal device usage belongs to you alone.

Information We Collect and How We Use It

Snap Out processes minimal data strictly required to deliver core app functionality:

  • Usage Statistics: The total number of “Snapouts” triggered and estimates of time saved.
  • Shielded App Preferences: The list of specific applications you configure the app to shield or limit.

All of this data is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted, synced, or uploaded to our servers, nor is it sent to external databases or third-party cloud services.

Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support ongoing development and offer premium features, Snap Out works with trusted third-party providers:

  • Google AdMob: Provides banner advertisements within the free version of the application. AdMob may process anonymous device identifiers (such as the Advertising Identifier / IDFA if consented) and coarse device telemetry to deliver non-personalized or personalized ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat & Apple In-App Purchases: Handles subscription verification and purchases for Snap Out Pro. Transaction IDs and receipt information are validated anonymously without linking to personal contact info.

Data Sharing and Selling

Snap Out does not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data, app preferences, or usage metrics with any third parties or advertising brokers.

Children’s Privacy

Snap Out is not directed at children under the age of 13. Because the application does not transmit or store personal data remotely, we do not knowingly solicit or collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated “Effective Date” at the top. Continued use of the application after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
